NSE Digital Academy & Virtual Trading Platform

Developed at the Nairobi Securities Exchange (NSE)

Two production web platforms designed, developed, and deployed at the Nairobi Securities Exchange to drive financial literacy and capital markets participation among young Kenyans and institutional audiences.

⚠️ Source code is confidential (NSE intellectual property). This repository documents the project scope, architecture, and outcomes.

What Was Built
NSE Digital Academy

An e-learning platform delivering financial literacy content — covering capital markets, investment principles, and stock market participation — to students, corporate employees, and the general public. Built to serve as NSE's primary digital education channel.

NSE Virtual Trading Platform

A simulated stock market trading environment allowing users to practise buying and selling listed securities using virtual portfolios. Used for onboarding new investors, training programmes at banks and schools, and Cellulant-style fintech partnerships.

Scale & Impact
5,000+ participants across schools, universities, banks, and corporate organisations in Kenya
Institutional partnerships with Strathmore University, ABSA, KCB, Co-operative Bank, Kingdom Bank, Jubilee, and I&M Bank
TradingView API integration — consuming real-time and historical market data via RESTful web services
Presented to and adopted by Agile Business Solutions and BAT as institutional platform partners
Technical Highlights
RESTful API consumption (TradingView market data integration)
User authentication, portfolio management, and progress tracking
Data pipelines for market data aggregation and validation
Python automation tools supporting platform operations (80% reduction in processing time)
Deployed and maintained in a production environment at a regulated financial market infrastructure
Role

Led end-to-end software development: requirements documentation, solution design, development, testing, deployment, and ongoing platform maintenance.
